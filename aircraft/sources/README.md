# Source ledger

Dated observations, paraphrased. Do not treat a product title or sales claim as an aircraft qualification. Prices can change. No vendor was contacted and no order was placed.

- **S01 — [MGM COMPRO REB 50](https://mgm-compro.com/electric-motor/reb-50-50-kw/)** (manufacturer, checked 2026-11-09): 12 kg; 50 kW peak; 30–40 kW continuous; 200 Nm maximum torque; 1500–4000 rpm; 400–800 V. Installation-dependent ratings. Price by quotation.
- **S02 — [MGM COMPRO HBCi 400800](https://mgm-compro.com/controllers-inverters-escs/hbci-400800/)** (manufacturer, checked 2026-11-09): 120–800 V; 4 kg cooling-plate version. Larger cooled versions weigh more. Specific cooling integration and price require supplier confirmation. Oversized for this concept.
- **S03 — [E-PROPS adjustable-pitch UAV range](https://uav.e-props.fr/adjustable-pitch.php)** (manufacturer, checked 2026-11-09): Ground-adjustable range for 50–180 kW engines. Matched CW/CCW diameter limited to 74.8 inches. Exact thrust, pitch, rpm, hub and aircraft match require supplier engineering; range membership does not establish compatibility.
- **S04 — [Molicel P50B product and datasheet v1.1](https://www.molicel.com/product/inr-21700-p50b/)** (manufacturer, checked 2026-11-09): 3.6 V nominal; 5.0 Ah typical; maximum cell mass 71 g; manufacturer page lists 60 A discharge. Cell-level ratings are not a complete pack qualification.
- **S05 — [Molicel P50B original datasheet](https://www.molicel.com/wp-content/uploads/4.TR%E7%B0%A1%E6%98%93%E8%A6%8F%E6%A0%BC_INR21700P50B_1.1_Product-Data-Sheet-of-INR-21700-P50B-80122.pdf)** (manufacturer, checked 2026-11-09): 18.0 Wh typical / 17.5 Wh minimum; 4.2 V charge; 12.8 mΩ typical DC impedance at 50% SOC; 60 A discharge with 80°C cutoff. Screening uses 40 A, 3.0 V OCV and constant resistance, not measured pack behavior.
- **S06 — [18650 Battery Store P50B retail/bulk listing](https://www.18650batterystore.com/products/molicel-21700-p50b-5000mah-50a-battery)** (seller, checked 2026-11-09): Opened page: $11.99 single cell; $7.50 at 1000+ quantity. Search excerpt showed a different promotion. Use the opened bulk tier for the dated cell-only cost, subject to checkout and stock confirmation.
- **S07 — [T-MOTOR U15XXL combination specifications and test table](https://store.tmotor.com/product/u15xxl-manned-aircraft-combo.html)** (manufacturer/seller, checked 2026-11-09): Motor listing $1999; Thunder 300A 24S $1599; NS62×24 listing $2599 (sales unit needs confirmation). NS62 test maximum is 101.449 kgf at 96 V, 209.09 A, 2203 rpm; coil temperature note 184°C at 32°C ambient after one minute at full throttle. This is not a continuous installed rating.
- **S08 — [E-PROPS aircraft prices (comparison only)](https://aircraft.e-props.fr/ordering.php)** (manufacturer/seller, checked 2026-11-09): Durandal/Excalibur two-blade listing €1304 excluding tax/shipping. This is not a quotation for the proposed matched UAV rotors and is not entered as their price.
- **S09 — [Holybro Pixhawk 6X](https://holybro.com/products/pixhawk-6x)** (manufacturer/seller, checked 2026-11-09): Opened Standard v2A / PM02D selection $422.99. Educational subscale/bench candidate only; the included power module is not rated for the concept HV bus. Not a qualified two-person flight-control system.
- **S10 — [NASA lift equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/lift-equation-2/)** (theory, checked 2026-11-09): Reference for L=ρV²SCL/2; airfoil coefficients remain assumptions.
- **S11 — [NASA NDARC theory appendix](https://rotorcraft.arc.nasa.gov/Publications/files/NASA%20TP-2009-215402-app3.pdf)** (theory, checked 2026-11-09): Reference background for rotor momentum and profile-power estimates. This repository is not NDARC and does not implement its complete rotor model.

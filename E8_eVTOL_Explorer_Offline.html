OPEN THE BLACK BOX

Every estimate has an assumption.

All calculations use SI units internally. Power is battery electrical power unless marked “shaft.” Values below update with the selected configuration.

01 / MASS & GEOMETRY

Close the mass budget first

m = mempty + 2mperson + mbattery + mwing
W = mg   ·   A = 8πd²/4

Empty mass includes motors, rotors, structure and systems, excluding battery, occupants and the optional wing. Wing mass is zero for wingless. Eight equal, non-overlapping disks share load; g = 9.80665 m/s².

02 / WING LIFT & DRAG

Let the wing carry its share

q = ½ρV²
CL,lim = CL,max/s²
L = min(W, qSCL,lim)
CL = L/(qS)
D = qf + qSCD0 + qSCL²/(πeAR)

S is wing area, f is the body equivalent flat-plate drag area (CDA), e is Oswald efficiency and AR is aspect ratio. s is your stall-speed margin. Wingless sets L and both wing drag terms to zero. Body f should include nacelles, booms and exposed hardware.

03 / ROTOR THRUST & INFLOW

Balance vertical and horizontal forces

Tz = W − L   ·   T = √(Tz² + D²)
α = atan2(D,Tz)
Va = V sin α   ·   Vp = V cos α
T = 2ρAvi√(Vp² + (Va + vi)²)

α is thrust tilt from vertical. The positive momentum-theory root vi is solved by 64 bisection steps. All eight rotors can tilt from vertical to horizontal and remain powered; full wing support still requires propulsive thrust D.

04 / SHAFT & ELECTRICAL POWER

Include induced, profile and drive losses

μ = Vp/Utip
P0,h[W] = (σcd/8)ρAUtip³
P0[W] = P0,h(1 + 3μ²)
Pshaft[kW] = (DV + κTvi + P0)/1000
Pelec[kW] = Pshaft/ηdrive + Paux[kW]

σ is blade solidity; cd is constant mean blade drag; κ corrects ideal induced power. The profile term is a screening approximation with constant tip speed, not a blade-element solution. No extra propeller efficiency is applied: inflow and profile losses already model aerodynamic power. Drive efficiency covers motor/inverter losses.

05 / HOVER & LIMITS

Check both the motors and the pack

vh = √(W/(2ρA))
Ph,shaft[kW] = (κWvh + P0,h)/1000
Ph[kW] = Ph,shaft/ηdrive + Paux[kW]
Pshaft,max[kW] = 8Pmotor[kW]
Pbatt,max[kW] = Crate Enom[kWh]

Both hover and cruise must meet shaft and pack limits. The reported hover thrust ceiling solves the hover power equation at the smaller of those limits; it is a power-only ceiling without blade loading, thermal or structural constraints. Range is withheld when power, energy or μ ≤ 0.35 screens fail. Wing-only stall speed is Vs = √(2W/(ρSCL,max)); multiply m/s by 3.6 for km/h.

06 / MISSION ENERGY & RANGE

Spend energy before you count range

Enom = mbatt ε / 1000
Eavail = Enom(SOC − reserve)/100
Efixed = Ph th/60 + Etransition
R[km] = max(0,Eavail − Efixed)/Pelec × V[km/h]
Etrip = Efixed + Pelec dtrip/V[km/h]

ε is pack-level Wh/kg, including packaging. SOC and reserve are percentages of nominal energy. Hover time in minutes combines takeoff and landing. Transition energy is your additional allowance for transition, climb and descent; it is not predicted. Cruise power and density are constant, with no wind, regeneration, aging or temperature derating.

Model domain and missing physics

Rotor diagnostics use CT/σ = T/(ρAUtip²σ), with T = W in hover. The rough hover mean blade lift coefficient is cℓ ≈ 6CT/σ. These are diagnostics, not pass criteria: this app has no rotor geometry or airfoil data to set a valid blade-stall or compressibility limit. High values can invalidate a result even when power and energy screens pass. The constant-drag profile approximation also omits the axial-flow correction.

This is a comparative concept model, not a flight-performance or certification tool. It does not resolve rotor interference, wing downwash in hover, blade stall, detailed rotor hub forces, axial-flow profile corrections, stability, control, rotor-out operation, noise, battery thermal behavior, or a full transition trajectory. More lift at low speed is limited by your CL input; the remaining weight is carried by rotors. A real fixed-rotor or lift-plus-cruise layout needs a different model. All starting numbers are assumptions, not measured properties of your aircraft.
